Indonesia Geography

Location and islands

The southernmost country in Asia, Indonesia lies between latitudes 11°S and 6°N and longitudes 95°E and 141°E. It spans Southeast Asia and Oceania and is the world's largest archipelagic state. Its five largest islands are Sumatra, Java, Borneo, Sulawesi, and New Guinea.

Climate

Indonesia's position near the equator brings warm conditions throughout the year. The dry season usually runs from May to October and the wet season from November to April. Highland areas are generally cooler and wetter than the lowlands.
